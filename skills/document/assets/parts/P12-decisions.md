# P12 Decisions and corrections

- Requirement: Required when deciding a shared contract or policy, or correcting existing facts or agreements
- Placement: Document and review records after the body
- Narrative role: Records detailed decision and correction history at the end.
- Author: Document owner and the relevant decision owner
- Reviewer: Both provider and consumer for shared contracts. The relevant decision maker for product, permission or operational policies. The owning team for technical fact corrections
- Check: Is there an actual choice or confirmation record? Are corrections and comparisons recorded only when there is previous content to change?
