# P03 Evidence notes

- Requirement: Conditionally required when actual material is cited. Omit when there is none; existing documents are not required
- Placement: Evidence notes at the very end of the document
- Narrative role: Places the sources, original material and limitations of body notes at the end.
- Author: Observer or investigator
- Reviewer: An owner on the responsible team with access to the code, operations or screen for each fact
- Check: Is the scope confirmed by direct observations, code, captures, logs and measurement records accurate? Do notes connect to actual citations without assuming unavailable material or past documents?
